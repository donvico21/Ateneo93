Who We Are

The ADNU Knights
of '93

We are the High School graduating class of 1993 of Ateneo de Naga University — a brotherhood shaped by Jesuit tradition, the spirit of the Golden Knights, and the heart of the Bicol region.

More than three decades on, the bonds of Batch '93 remain unbreakable. From classrooms and corridors to careers and families across the globe, these brothers carry the ADNU spirit wherever they go.

"Ad Majorem Dei Gloriam" — for the greater glory of God — the motto that guided us then and continues to inspire us now. Above all, we are called to be Men and Women for Others.
